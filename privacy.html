Swipe Strike – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

In short: Swipe Strike has no accounts and does not collect your name, email or any personal details. The game saves its settings and your purchases on your own device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play. Online matches connect you directly to the other player.

Who we are

Swipe Strike ("the game") is made by Cory Syx ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at syxofficial@gmail.com.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves a small amount of information on your phone so it works the way you left it. This stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your settings (sound, music, screen effects, controls and similar options).
  • Which items you have bought (Remove Ads and costumes), and when a "gold colours" reward from a watched ad runs out.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this information. Purchases can be restored from Google Play with the "Restore Purchases" button in the shop.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads between matches using Google AdMob. Ads are never shown during a fight, in training or in online matches. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID.
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information (for example device model, operating system and language).
  • How you interact with the ads you see.

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy Settings" button in the game's shop. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google acts independently when it processes this data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teenagers and older.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases (Remove Ads and costumes) are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which items you own, so it can unlock them. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Online matches

When you play online, the game connects your device directly to your opponent's device. To make this connection:

  • A connection service (PeerJS) is used briefly to introduce the two devices, using a temporary match code or random connection ID. Like any internet service, it sees your device's IP address while doing this.
  • Because the connection is direct, your opponent's device can see your IP address, and yours can see theirs. This is normal for peer-to-peer online games.
  • If a direct connection is not possible (for example on some mobile networks), match data is passed through a relay server run on Cloudflare. The relay only forwards game data between the two players.

Online matches do not use accounts, names or chat. The only information exchanged is game data, such as your chosen fighter, costume, colour and your in-match inputs.

Children

Swipe Strike is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK or EU data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. We do not hold personal data about you on our own systems. For data processed by Google for ads or purchases, you can use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com, or contact us and we will help where we can. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Security

Game data is sent using standard encrypted connections where available. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the information the game uses to the minimum it needs.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game gains new features. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date at the top.
